Case study

SignalFlow

AIWorkspace Released Jul 18, 2026
Role

Lead product designer

Timeline

12 weeks

Team

Design + 4 engineers

Deliverables

UX flows · Design system · Framer prototype

Overview

SignalFlow asked for a single workspace where operations teams could build, supervise and trust their AI automations. What started as a dashboard brief turned into a full product language for human-in-the-loop AI.

The challenge

Automation pipelines were powerful but opaque. Teams couldn't tell why a run failed, who approved it, or what would happen next — so they bypassed the tool entirely. The interface had to make machine behaviour legible without dumbing it down.

The approach

We mapped every automation state with the team, then designed a run timeline that reads like a story: trigger, steps, decisions, outcomes. I prototyped the supervision flow in Framer and tested it with operators weekly, tightening the information hierarchy each round.

The result

Supervision time dropped by 40%, and teams began shipping automations they previously ran by hand. The design system we built now powers every new SignalFlow surface.
